Severity encoding bake-off

Six static treatments of one record set. Scores use rubric v1.1; the observed distribution is a random sample of 90 records from the 7,855-record archive.

The presentation problem

Every sampled score is 22 plus a scale band, while remediation and notification lag are unassessable from the source. The treatments below keep the honest denominator, make the narrow present-day range legible, distinguish missing assessment from earned zero, and leave room for future scores across the full rubric.

The supplied register is used without additions. No new color value or hue is introduced; solid, hatched, positional, numeric, and textual cues carry meaning in light mode, dark mode, and monochrome print.

Recommendation

Ship Variant 6 because it gives records affected the logarithmic treatment its four-order range needs while preserving severity as an independent, honest 0–100 measure. Its divided point makes partial assessability inseparable from the score, and its two axes will become more informative rather than obsolete when remediation and lag begin to vary. If table height is non-negotiable, Variant 4 is the restrained fallback, but the orthogonal plot is the only option that fully exposes today’s redundancy and tomorrow’s divergence.
